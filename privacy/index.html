Privacy

What this site does with information. Effective 23 October 2026.

Movementizer is published by KHARD HRIDAYA Inc. This policy describes the site at movementizer.com, including the same pages shown in the Movementizer app. It matches what the site actually does today.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, or GPS location. There is no account and no payment. The pages do not run an advertising or analytics script. They do not set cookies.

The address bar

Opening a movement window changes the page address to movementizer.com/?w= and the movement's id, for example ?w=nokings. That id is already printed on the page. We use it only to reopen the same window. We do not send it to a tracking service.

Files your browser loads from others

To draw the page, your browser requests files from a few other sites. Those sites see the request, which can include your IP address. We do not receive a copy of those requests.

  • Typefaces come from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com).
  • Photos come from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org). The credit on each photo links to Wikimedia and to the license.
  • Video stills come from YouTube (i.ytimg.com) on the radar and inside a window, before anything plays.

A video file does not load until you press play. Play then places a player from youtube-nocookie.com. On a website, YouTube calls that privacy-enhanced mode: a view there is not used to personalize later browsing on YouTube, or ads outside this site. YouTube still receives the play request and can collect data under its own rules. YouTube says this mode is for players on websites, not for a player inside an app view. The same play button is what the app uses, so treat playback there as YouTube's own collection. The page also links to the same video on youtube.com.

Source lists link out to news outlets and other sites. If you follow a link, that site's own policy applies.

Share

Each movement window has a Share button. We do not keep a record of what you share.

  • On the website, Share via device hands the title, a short sentence, and the window link to an app you choose on your device.
  • In the iOS app, Share opens the system share sheet. The app does not keep a record of it.
  • Facebook and X open with the window link in the address, so those companies receive that link if you continue there.
  • Instagram and TikTok do not receive the link from us. The link is copied, then their site opens so you can paste it yourself.
  • Copy link writes the window address to your clipboard. It is not sent to us.

From the ground

Each movement window can hold clips that people record or upload. A clip is not public until a person approves it. You can post with a random handle. A real name is not required.

On this device we save three things in local storage: a random id, the handle, and the time you accepted the terms. A list of posters you block stays on this device too. The app stores the same kind of choice for alerts, which is separate and described below.

When you post, we store the clip, the movement id, the handle, the random id, the caption, the time, and the review status. If you tick the place box and type a place name, we store that text. If you do not, we store no place. We do not ask the phone for GPS, and we do not read a location out of the file to put it on a map.

A photo is drawn again in the browser or the app before it is sent, which drops the camera's hidden location data. A video you record in the page or the app is a new file. A video you upload from the camera roll is the file you chose. We do not open it to collect coordinates. If that original file still has location data inside it, record in the page or the app instead of uploading the original.

The file is stored in a private Vercel Blob store. The details are stored in a Neon Postgres database. While a clip is waiting, only you and the moderators can open the file. After it is approved, the site can show it. If it is rejected or banned, we delete the file.

If an AI Gateway key is configured, we send a still frame and the caption to Vercel AI Gateway so a vision model can flag sexual content, gore, spam, and clips that are not about the movement. Violence in a protest is flagged for a person, not rejected on its own. If no key is set, that check is skipped and the clip stays pending. The model provider sees the still for that check.

Report stores your random id, the clip id, and the reason you write. Three reports from different people hide an approved clip. Block only hides a poster on your device.

The app

The iOS app shows this same site. Links that leave movementizer.com open in the phone's browser. Record uses the camera and the microphone. Upload uses the photo library. The app asks for those only when you tap Record or Upload. Alerts are off until you turn them on. If you do, the app stores two things on the phone only: that the choice is on, and the last status it saw for each window (Live, Still on, or Fulfilled). When you open the app, it reads the public page and, if a status changed, shows a notification on this device. It does not send that list to us. There is no push server, and nothing is checked while the app is closed.

Hosting

The site is served by Vercel. Delivering a page means Vercel can see ordinary request data: IP address, date and time, the address you opened, and the browser you used. We do not use those logs to recognize you on a later visit or to build a profile. How the host handles that data is covered by Vercel's own privacy policy.

Children

The registry is public. The ground feed is not for anyone under 17. Do not post a clip if you are under 17. We do not knowingly keep a clip from a child. If you believe we have one, email us and we will remove it.

Changes

If the site starts collecting something new, we will update this page and change the date at the top. An old sentence will not be left in place to cover a new behavior.

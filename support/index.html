Support

Questions and corrections for the Earth radar.

Movementizer is published by KHARD HRIDAYA Inc. Write to hridaya.8888@gmail.com. We read the mail. There is no phone line and no chat account on the site.

A window looks wrong

Name the movement, say what is wrong, and send a source we can read. The radar is corrected from published reports. A note without a source is harder to place.

What email cannot do

We cannot take a video off YouTube, a photo off Wikimedia Commons, or a story off a news site. Those belong to the people who published them. We can correct our own window.

A clip from the ground

Use Report on the clip. To ask for a removal, or to report something the button does not cover, email hridaya.8888@gmail.com with the movement name and what is wrong. We read the mail. Objectionable content is not allowed, and a clip that breaks the terms can be removed and the poster banned.

The app

The app shows this same site. If a page will not open, email us with the device, the system version, and what you tapped.
